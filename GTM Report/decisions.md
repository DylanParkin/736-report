# Decisions

## Initiation
- will be primarily user-triggered through use of button, but will also have the ability to assess if vital signs (SpO2, RR, HR) are well outside the reference ranges. At this stage, not entirely decided on the hybrid/automatic aspect, as this device is intended mainly for exercising adults, so their reference range may fluctuate widely.

## Recovery
- The device is intended to be used by exercising people, but the recovery stage/exercises are intended to be done stationary.

## Exclusions
- The device is not intended to contact emergency services.
- The device is not intended to do any diagnosis.
- The device is not intended to do autonomous treatment; the device is instead meant to be used in conjunction with regular clincian/physio checkups where they can prescribe/programme exercises to the device.

## Form factor
- Intended to be wrist worn
- intended to be relatively low form factor
- meant to have haptic feedback as primary form of feedback to users
- meant to have PPG sensors in sensing suite (although why/why not is not fully clear right now)

## App status
- unsure, but at the moment it is not meant to have a dedicated app for the user - this is under decision
- The probable interface will be a clinician only one, for when the device is physically present.
