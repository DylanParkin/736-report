## COPD Atlas
- 66,000 of PHO enrolled 45+ years had COPD in 2023 (up from 20,000 in 2018) - more diagnosis/medicine prescribed, not more prevelance
- Maori most (11.8%), asian least (1.2%).
- ~half (48.6%) regularly get triple therapy (LAMA/LABA/ICS) - consistent over time, females slightly higher.
- limitations from article:
    - underrep of mild copd that doesn't get hospitalisation - important for project as this is probably a fairly significant portion of our target pop.
    - Doesn't get people with asthma-copd overlap
- about 11.1% of pho enrolled pop 45+ years admitted to hospital 1+ times with copd diagnosis as primary (down from 16.3% in 2018)
- People in rural areas may face additional challenges (lower income, reduced access to healthcare) impacting diagnosis, treatment and management.


## Health and Indepenednece Report 2025

- estimated pop at 31 december 2025 = 5,344,500

- Population aging: old-age dependency ratio is 1 in 4 (2024) -> 1 in 3 over next 20 years.

life expectancy at birth = 80.4 for males, 83.7 for femails.

respiratory diseases = 2,981 in 2023 (third highest cause of death in nz)

proportion of disability adjusted life years lost due to chronic respiratory disease = 5.5% (7th highest cause)
- Most commone condition in this group is COPD (2.9% of total health loss) followed by asthma (2.1% of total health loss).
- Chronic resp diseases contribute to substantial mortality, morbidity, and health service use.

**94,000 adults 45+ years old** have copd (4.4% prevalence). Maori 2.07 times more likely than non-maori.

Slightly higher in women (4.8%) then men (3.9%). 

11.6% of disabled 45+ year old adults have copd.
Intellecutally disabled people 1.5 times more likely than non.
