\section{Detailed Design}

This section presents the detailed mechatronic design of the proposed wearable device, building on the conceptual design outlined in Section III. The design is structured around several core aspects: the mechanical design of the wearable enclosure and its interface with the wrist, the electrical schematic and sensor circuitry required for physiological signal acquisition, the underlying software/control logic governing the device's operation, and the overall system architecture that integrates sensing, processing, and feedback into a cohesive device. Each of these aspects is discussed in turn, with the accompanying design rationale grounded in the requirements and constraints established in the preceding sections.

\subsection{Mechanical Design}

The wearable adopts a standard wristwatch form factor, comprising a single moulded housing and an adjustable strap to accommodate varying wrist sizes. As feedback is delivered exclusively through haptic actuation rather than a visual display, the design omits a watch face, simplifying the enclosure to a single sealed electronics compartment. TPU is used here, being a flexible, durable material commonly used in wearable enclosures. At the area of skin contact housing the PPG sensor, a clear polycarbonate window is integrated to permit optical transmission while protecting the underlying sensor from sweat and mechanical wear. Given the intended use during physical exercise, the device targets an IP67 ingress protection rating to ensure resistance to sweat and moisture. To achieve this while minimising assembly complexity, the housing is to be joined via ultrasonic welding, creating a continuous, seam-free seal without requiring additional sealing components such as O-rings or gaskets. The polycarbonate window is bonded to the housing using a UV-cured optical adhesive. Overall, the mechanical design follows established conventions for wrist-worn wearable devices, prioritising manufacturability and a proven form factor for user comfort. \figref{fig:mechanical-design} shows an exploded view of the housing assembly, identifying the key mechanical and sealing components.
